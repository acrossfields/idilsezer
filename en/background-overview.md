<div class="bgsum">
  I was born in Istanbul, Türkiye. I completed middle school at FMV Işık Schools and attended Pertevniyal High School. I studied Aeronautical Engineering at Istanbul Technical University, earning both my bachelor's and master's degrees, before joining GE Aviation as a Design Engineer, a position I held for the next four years. I later moved to São Paulo, Brazil, where I spent three years teaching English at various institutions, followed by five years working in video game translation and quality assurance, and acquired Brazilian citizenship. Alongside my professional work, I explored other fields, including wind energy and programming, through independent online study. I then returned to Istanbul and completed a certificate program in Python Programming and Data Science offered by Istanbul Technical University in collaboration with Mindset Institute. I currently work as an R&D Engineer at MNM Teknoloji.
